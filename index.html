About the Department

Who We Are

We are a passionate team of educators and learners committed to excellence in computer science education. Our curriculum blends theory, projects, and practical exposure.

Mission:
To develop technically sound, ethically strong, and industry-ready graduates.

  • Established
    2008
  • HOD
    Prof. Anita Sharma
  • Accreditation
    NAAC A+
  • Affiliation
    Mumbai University

Our department stands out for its experienced faculty, industry-relevant curriculum, modern lab facilities, and consistent student placement records. We focus on both academic excellence and hands-on training.

The department has won multiple intercollegiate coding competitions, published over 50+ research papers, and has active MoUs with leading tech companies for training and internships.

We offer a modern programming lab, IoT lab, cloud computing infrastructure, fully air-conditioned classrooms, and campus-wide 24x7 Wi-Fi.

Graduates can pursue careers in software development, data science, AI/ML, cybersecurity, web development, and research. Many students also choose to pursue higher studies or certifications to specialize further.

Our department collaborates with companies like Infosys, TCS, AWS Educate, Red Hat Academy, and Wipro through workshops, internships, and certification programs.
